About PneumoScan AI

PneumoScan AI was developed by a team of Developers and AI researchers to address the growing need for accurate, rapid pneumonia detection, particularly in resource-constrained settings.

Our technology is trained on over 100,000 annotated chest X-rays from diverse populations, ensuring robust performance across different patient demographics.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is PneumoScan AI compared to human radiologists?

In clinical trials, PneumoScan AI demonstrated 98.5% accuracy in detecting pneumonia, comparable to senior radiologists. However, it's designed to work alongside radiologists, not replace them, combining AI precision with human clinical judgment.

Is patient data secure when using PneumoScan AI?

Absolutely. We use HIPAA-compliant encryption for all data transfers and storage. Patient images are anonymized before processing, and you maintain full control over your data.

What file formats does PneumoScan AI support?

We support both DICOM (preferred) and standard image formats (JPEG, PNG). Our system automatically adjusts for variations in image quality and resolution.

Can PneumoScan AI differentiate between different types of pneumonia?

Our current version identifies pneumonia presence and location. Version 2.0 (coming Q3 2023) will include classification of bacterial vs. viral pneumonia with 92% preliminary accuracy.
